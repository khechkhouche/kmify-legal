3What we do not do

  • No advertising in the app.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No advertising trackers.
  • Nothing is sent to the cloud without your action: backup and sharing are manual.

4Technical processors

Firebase (Google) provides authentication, verification and password-reset e-mails, cloud backup and crash reports (Crashlytics); Google Play handles payments and the review prompt (no data is sent to Kmify for it). See Google's privacy policy.

Verification and password-reset e-mails may land in your spam folder: please check it.

5Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Account, cloud backup and share data are kept as long as your account exists.

6Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

  • Export: the app's “Local backup” feature.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete my account.
  • Request: kmifypro@gmail.com.
  • Complaint: your national data protection authority (e.g. CNIL in France).

7Security

Exchanges with Firebase are encrypted. Only the account holder can read or edit their own cloud backup. A share code only gives read-only access to summary figures.

8Minors

The app is not aimed at minors and does not knowingly collect data about people under 15.

9Changes and contact

This policy may change; the update date is shown at the top of the page. Questions: kmifypro@gmail.com.
