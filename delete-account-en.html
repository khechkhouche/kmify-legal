1From the app Immediate

  1. Open Kmify and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings, “Sensitive zone”.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Optionally tick “Also erase all data on this device”, then confirm.
  5. Confirm your identity: password, or Google sign-in depending on how you registered.
